# Governance

- Maintainer: wpexpertid. Decisions are recorded in `docs/decisions/` as ADRs.
- Versioning: semver. A minor release every 6 to 8 weeks, an LTS line every 12 months with 18 months of security fixes.
- Upstream: PocketBase security advisories are reviewed within 7 days and ported when applicable (`docs/COMPAT.md` lists the pinned version).
- Contributions: DCO sign-off (`git commit -s`), MIT, no CLA.
- Breaking changes to the PocketBase contract require an entry in `docs/COMPAT.md` and a migration note.
